## Researched outline

1. Define the job: implementation, design/build, or brand/website; distinguish generic expertise from Certified Partner status.
2. Discovery routes: official Webflow Partners, Upwork, Contra, Dribbble, Behance; comparison of what each helps evaluate.
3. Vetting: live work, actual role, responsive behavior, CMS, technical judgment, communication, handoff; flat Figma checklist image and semantic list.
4. Real evidence: NextDimension identity and website images, case study, live site; explicit Elevate implementation attribution.
5. Budget: attributed Upwork experience-level examples, explain conflicting headline range; Arek's verified engagement pricing; scope and recurring costs.
6. Delivery: written brief, design approval, build, review, handoff; deliverables and responsibilities.
7. FAQs and direct portfolio/book-a-call CTA, with verified platform profiles.
