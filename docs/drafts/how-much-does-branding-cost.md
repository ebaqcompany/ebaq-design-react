## Start with scope, not a dollar number

Before you compare prices, name the job.

| Job | What you are buying | Rough shape |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Logo only | A mark and file package | See [logo design cost](/blog/logo-design-cost) |
| Core identity | Logo system, type, color, usable guidelines | Sprint / package territory |
| Identity + website | Brand system plus a marketed site | Two scopes (or a combined package) |
| Full rebrand program | Strategy, identity, rollout, often web + collateral over months | Agency / long engagement |

If two quotes differ by 5×, they are almost never the same product.

## What $3,000 actually buys

The Brand Sprint is for founders past DIY and marketplace roulette who need a **core identity**, not a six-month agency program.

**In about one week, the core engagement typically covers:**

- Custom logo system (primary mark and practical lockups)
- Color palette and typography direction
- An online style guide your team can use
- Key brand assets agreed for the sprint
- Direct work with me — senior designer, no junior handoff
- Revisions inside the sprint scope

Homepage framing, same idea: *custom logo, type & colors, style guide & key brand assets, work directly with Arek*.

**It is not** research theater with three account layers.

**It is** a focused identity you can put on a site, deck, and product UI without guessing hex codes in Slack.

## What $3k does not buy

Usually **out of** the core Brand Sprint unless we scope them separately:

- Full marketing website design/build (Framer, Webflow, or React)
- Large illustration or photography production
- Packaging, vehicles, environmental graphics
- Heavy motion beyond what we agree for the sprint
- Unlimited multi-quarter retainers (that is the **$10k/mo** path)

If you need brand **and** site together, use a buyer checklist like [branding and website redesign scope](/blog/branding-and-website-redesign-scope) and book a call so both are sized honestly.

Peers who publish brand+web bands (Proof of Work **$25–35k**, PIXELUP **$25–40k**) are selling a **wider** job than a one-week identity sprint — again, compare scopes.

## How to compare any branding quote

Force every proposal onto one page:

1. **Deliverables** — logo only, identity system, messaging, website, guidelines depth?
2. **Who does the work** — named senior, or juniors behind an account manager?
3. **Timeline** — calendar weeks to usable files.
4. **Revisions and ownership** — limits, source files, usage rights.
5. **What is explicitly out** — so "$X branding" does not secretly mean logo-only.

## FAQ

### How much does branding cost for a small business?

Enough to buy a **usable identity system**, not just a JPEG.

Ebaq's core Brand Sprint is **$3,000** for about one week.

Agency programs on our Comparison commonly sit at **$25k–$100k+**.

Logo-only budgets belong in the [logo design cost](/blog/logo-design-cost) guide.

### Is $3,000 enough for a real brand identity?

For core identity — logo system, type, color, guide, key assets — yes, when scope stays honest and the designer is senior and direct.

It is not enough if you also expect a full custom website, packaging line, and campaign system in the same week.

### Why do some brand sprints cost $15k or $50k?

Different studios package different teams, research depth, and inclusions.

- Proof of Work states **$15,000 flat / 2 weeks** for their Brand Sprint.
- PIXELUP states a Brand Sprint **starting at $15,000** (~2 weeks on their cost blog).
- Primary states a flat **$50k / 2 weeks**.

Those are **competitor-stated** offers for **their** scopes — useful for mapping the market, not a claim every business needs that band.

### What about Metabrand's $4,490 packages?

Metabrand publishes exact SKUs on their [startup offer](https://www.metabrand.digital/startup-offer): Brand Identity **$4,490**, Website **$4,490**, Complete **$8,390**.

They also publish separate FAQ engagement ranges (about **$15k–$65k** on their [tech branding](https://www.metabrand.digital/tech-branding-agency) page).

Do not collapse those into one "average." Compare the SKU line items to what you need.

### Does Slam publish a fixed brand-sprint price?

No fixed public sprint sticker on their rebrand service page.

Their content cites typical startup brand engagements around **$15k–$50k** ([startup brand agency](https://www.slammedialab.com/post/startup-brand-agency)), with focused rebrands often **8–16 weeks** ([rebranding agency](https://www.slammedialab.com/services/rebranding-agency)) — useful range language, not a SKU.

### Should I hire an agency or a senior freelancer?

Hire the **smallest team that can own the work end-to-end**.

If you want speed, taste, and one throat to choke, a senior sprint beats a multi-month agency for most early-stage brands.

If you need enterprise process across many workstreams, agency math can make sense — budget for **$25k–$100k+** and months.

### Can I start with branding and add a website later?

Yes.

The $3,000 core identity can stand alone; website is additional scope.

Many clients ship identity first, then build Framer/Webflow/React next.

### How do I know if I need a rebrand vs a refresh before I spend?

Use [Should You Rebrand?](/blog/should-i-rebrand) before you commission anything.

A new identity will not fix an unclear offer.
