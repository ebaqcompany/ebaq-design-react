## What the evidence showed

- **AI baseline.** On November 20, logged-out ChatGPT named and linked Ebaq in 2 of 20 single runs, both about speed (03 and 17). It omitted Ebaq on the broad AI agency prompt (10), which named The Branx, Clay, Koto, Red Antler, Focus Lab, Smith & Diction, and Brightscout. The sprint-vs-agency prompt (12) ran without web search. Browser access was unavailable for a comparable fresh run on November 22.
- **Miss diagnosis.** [The Branx](https://thebranx.com/faqs) publishes its price, process, and what needs a separate scope. [Dribbble’s startup agency comparison](https://dribbble.com/resources/agencies/branding-agencies-for-startups) names The Branx for AI and SaaS and describes budget, stage, and proof. This identifies a credible coverage gap to test. The November 20 run did not record Dribbble as the citation for prompt 10.
- **Google demand.** Google Keyword Planner, US English, collected November 22, estimates 140 monthly searches for “saas branding,” 50 for “saas branding agency,” 390 for “startup branding agency,” 70 for “brand sprint,” and 260 for “saas web design.” These are rounded search estimates. Metrics for “ai startup branding” and “brand sprint vs branding agency” were unavailable.
- **Google visibility.** Search Console Web, October 22 through November 18, all countries. The query “saas branding” had 295 impressions and 0 clicks on [/blog/saas-logos](https://www.ebaqdesign.com/blog/saas-logos), at average position 15.3. Page totals across all queries were 1 impression and 0 clicks for /saas-branding, 11 and 0 for /ai-startup-branding, and 3 and 0 for /brand-sprint.
- **Technical check.** Search Console URL Inspection reported /saas-branding, /ai-startup-branding, /saas-web-design, /brand-sprint, and /blog/brand-sprint-vs-agency as indexed. Public startup pages and their linked case studies returned HTTP 200 and self-referencing canonicals in raw HTML. The startup pages already had readable content and appeared in the sitemap.

## Readback after release

1. Record the deploy date and Google’s next crawl of each changed URL. Check the live canonical, rendered offer and FAQ text, case-study and review links, and the new article link. Retain the same three Google page totals and the exact “saas branding” query-by-page for a 28-day comparison.
2. Run the six prompts twice each on logged-out ChatGPT and a second available engine. Record date, engine, search shown, recommended provider, Ebaq mention, exact cited URLs, and whether the answer states the offer accurately. Record answers without search separately. Retest after the pages have been recrawled, then monthly. Rerun the full original 20 as a separate comparison if reporting change against the 2-of-20 baseline.
3. Track visits from AI referrers where visible, then [confirmed bookings](../booking-measurement.md), qualified calls, and wins. Note buyer-reported discovery on the call, since referrers can be absent. Treat every answer metric as a diagnostic and judge business value by qualified inquiries and wins.

For the next iteration, follow the repeated miss. If the answer misstates scope, find the cited source and correct the conflicting information you control. If it recommends peers via named reviews or roundups, focus on independent coverage. If it cites Ebaq but buyers still struggle to evaluate fit, inspect the page and the booking journey. The current intervention remains unproven until readback.
