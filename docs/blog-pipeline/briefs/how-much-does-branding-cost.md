### Keyword / SERP / AEO notes

**Primary KW: `how much does branding cost`**
- Top results emphasize **wide ranges** ($500–$500k) then **tier tables** by provider (DIY / freelancer / mid-studio / premium) and by scope (logo / core identity / full system / brand+web).
- Strong AEO pattern: stage budgets (pre-seed lean → Series A $15–30k+ → enterprise six figures), FAQs (“how much for a startup,” logo vs identity, timeline 4–8 weeks).
- Example SERP peers: Splash Creative mid-market tables ($5–30k studios; core identity $5–12k; full system $12–25k; brand+web $15–35k); topbrandagencies / dp.vision similar tier maps.
- AI-overview-friendly structure: definition of what “branding” includes → cost by who you hire → cost by scope → what drives price → FAQ.

**Secondary: `startup rebrand cost`**
- Dominated by **stage × $ maps** (POW’s own article ranks; PIXELUP stage table; Slam stage budgets).
- Common claimed bands in SERP synthesis (competitor content, not Ebaq claims): seed ~$15–40k, Series A higher, Series B $60–200k+.
- POW plants **$15k sprint** as the funded-startup answer; PIXELUP plants **$15k / $25–40k with site** and argues against $75–250k “market” guidance for Series A.

**Secondary: `brand sprint cost`**
- Category contested: fixed offers from **~$2.5k lean** up through **$15k senior sprint** to **$40–50k premium sprint**.
- POW owns a lot of this SERP with “what is a brand sprint” + brand-sprint product page at **$15k / 2 weeks**.
- AEO angle: define sprint vs full rebrand; fixed price + fixed weeks; what is / isn’t included.

**Gap Ebaq can own:** Almost no top result plants a **transparent senior-direct ~$3k / ~1 week** core identity option. Peers’ “affordable” floor in tables is usually freelancer $2–8k or Metabrand’s $4,490 package — but those are different scopes/teams. Ebaq differentiates on **senior direct + clear $3k/1-week** without pretending mid-tier sprint peers are cheaper than they state.

### Suggested Ebaq angle (~$3k / 1 week)

1. **Lead with scope clarity:** “Branding cost” = four different jobs (logo / core identity / identity+web / multi-month rebrand). Compare scopes, not totals.
2. **Plant the public offer accurately:** Ebaq Brand Sprint **~$3,000 / ~1 week**, senior-direct (Arek) — already in draft and site Comparison/FAQ. Do not invent volumes or ROI %.
3. **Use competitor-stated prices as calibration, not strawmen:**
   - POW **$15k / 2 weeks** (and $25–35k with web)
   - PIXELUP **$15k** brand sprint (2 weeks per blog)
   - Primary **$50k / 2 weeks**
   - Metabrand **$4,490** published Brand Identity SKU (and separate $15k–$65k engagement FAQ — don’t conflate)
   - Slam **$15–50k** typical startup range, no fixed sprint sticker
4. **Positioning sentence:** You are not “cheaper than everyone by magic” — you sell a **focused senior identity sprint** at a price peers’ public pages leave open between freelancer and $15k studio sprints.
5. **Cross-links:** logo-design-cost (logo-only), branding-package (what’s inside), should-i-rebrand / brand-refresh (whether to spend), /start CTA.
6. **Avoid:** inventing keyword volumes; blending Metabrand’s $4,490 SKU with their $15k+ FAQ into one fake “average”; attributing Slam a fixed $X sprint; claiming AI Overview content we didn’t see verbatim.
