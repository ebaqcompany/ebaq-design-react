# Law-firm logos first-nine showcase

## Final order

01. Nielsons Lawyers — `/images/blog/law-firm-logos/legacy/01-nielsons-lawyers.jpeg` — Ryan Romanes Studio with Matt Zwartz. Legacy source: Neilsons official site and Ryan Romanes Studio Behance project. The blue calligraphic emblem modernizes the previous monogram with a more distinctive cobalt silhouette.

02. Vesha Law — `/images/blog/law-firm-logos/legacy/02-vesha-law.jpeg` — For Brands with Marzin Kaczmarek. Legacy source: Vesha Law official site and For Brands Behance project. The VL construction and copper/silver foil treatment make a compact identity feel considered in both digital and tactile applications.

03. Grindal Legal — `/images/blog/law-firm-logos/legacy/03-grindal-legal.jpeg` — Both Studio. Legacy source: Grindal Legal official site and Both Studio Behance project. The restrained mark, open spacing, and contemporary color direction give a property and estate-planning practice a younger visual voice without sacrificing clarity.

04. Fox Berman — existing case-study assets from `LawFirmLanding.tsx`, including the Fox Berman logo identity and business-card imagery — Arek / EBAQ Design. Project route: `/work/fox-berman`. The boutique law-firm identity in Sint Maarten uses an all-caps wordmark with distinctive internal cuts and traps, paired with a bright orange accent connected to its Caribbean context.

05. Lexelis — `/images/blog/law-firm-logos/legacy/05-lexelis.jpeg` — Eskader. Legacy source: Lexelis official site and Eskader project. The serif monogram, contemporary letterform, and copper foil on black-and-white stock show how material contrast can add authority without visual clutter.

06. Judit Gyolcs — `/images/blog/law-firm-logos/legacy/06-judit-gyolcs.jpeg` — de_form studio with Eniko Deri and Nora Demeczky. Legacy source: Judit Gyolcs Law Firm and the credited Budapest studio team. The grid-led identity uses a precise symbol and straightforward typography to make a boutique practice feel individual rather than ornamental.

07. Von Wobeser — `/images/blog/law-firm-logos/legacy/07-von-wobeser.jpeg` — branding people/design studio. Legacy source: Von Wobeser official site and credited Mexico City studio. Red tones replace the expected legal blue, while foil and embossing extend the identity’s modern-traditional balance into physical touchpoints.

08. King & Collins — `/images/blog/law-firm-logos/legacy/08-king-collins.jpeg` — Pop & Pac Studio. Legacy source: King & Collins official site and Pop & Pac Studio project. The dual name treatment and measured composition create a calm, capable tone that feels designed rather than decorated.

09. Walless — `/images/blog/law-firm-logos/legacy/09-walless.jpeg` — Folk Design Agency with Regina Terekė, Ignas Kozlovas, and Evaldas Bubinas. Legacy source: Walless official site and credited Vilnius studio project. The modular mark and color system turn the idea of structural support into a flexible identity language.
